# Practice, contact control, depth-related loading and hand use: plan written before these results

Date: 2026-11-06. Author: Claude. Status: exploratory, not registered. Groups are pooled, with group difference reported as a check.
No untrained control exists and training levels were always in the order L1 to L5, so "practice" cannot be separated from time, repeated exposure or fatigue.

## Q1. Practice: did performance change from before to after?
Paired tests across subjects, matched levels: completion time, collisions, log TSSF, log TSSV. Elastic task Baseline1 to Baseline2, and Peg Transfer Test1 to Test2.
Elastic task per level, 5 paired tests. Within training: first 3 vs last 3 repetitions per level.

## Q2. Movement awareness. NOT directly measured. Proxy only: contact control.
Change from before to after in: collisions per minute, peak impact per collision (NIDAQ, g), force rate (TSSF per minute), and vibration rate (TSSV per minute).
Rates matter because trials became shorter. Closing-survey "aware of contacts" is reported descriptively. No pre-training rating exists.

## Q3. Depth perception. NOT directly measured. Proxy only: direction of board loading.
Assumption to confirm: sensor Fz is perpendicular to the board. Per trial: Fz share = energy in Fz / energy in Fx+Fy+Fz; Fz rate per minute; lateral rate per minute; peak |Fz|.
Pre vs post and across levels. This cannot separate depth perception from motor control, strategy or task geometry: no tool trajectory or stereoacuity test exists.

## Q4. Hand use
Per trial and tool, from accelerometers. Movement = 0.5-5 Hz magnitude; vibration = 80-1000 Hz energy.
- Asymmetry AI = (R-L)/(R+L) for movement and vibration.
- Dominant-referenced AI = AI x sign(handedness LQ). Mixed or LQ = 0 subjects are excluded from this measure only.
- Coordination: zero-lag correlation of left and right movement envelopes; share of time one hand moves at least 3x the other.
Tests: (a) is mean AI different from zero, (b) Spearman AI vs LQ, (c) does the more active tool match the questionnaire hand (count, with binomial test), (d) pre vs post change in dominant-referenced AI, |AI|, correlation and one-hand share, elastic and peg; (e) first 3 vs last 3 training repetitions.
Task layout could favour one side, so a side bias alone is not evidence of preference; LQ association and left-handers are the informative parts. Only 1 left-hander and 3 mixed exist, so left-hand results are descriptive.

## Data rules
Force and vibration use existing exclusion lists, and s16N is out of collision measures outside Test3. Test3 is excluded: opposite feedback condition, different goal and truncated NIDAQ.
BH within each of Q1 to Q4. Subject is the unit of inference. No extra measures, bands or subgroups after seeing results; later additions are dated here.
